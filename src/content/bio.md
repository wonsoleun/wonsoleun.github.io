I am a Bachelor of Engineering student in Artificial Intelligence, passionate about **Physical AI, robotics, and intelligent systems**. I am particularly interested in building AI that can perceive, reason, and act in the real world.

## Research Interests

- Embodied AI
- Generalist Robotic Policies
- Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models
- Imitation Learning & Reinforcement Learning

I am interested in building generalist robotic agents that can perceive, reason, and act in diverse real-world environments. I am currently exploring Vision-Language-Action (VLA) foundation models and how well they generalize across tasks and environments. I'm also interested in imitation learning and reinforcement learning as complementary approaches to improving robotic policy learning and adaptability.

## Current Work

I am exploring the intersection of artificial intelligence, robotics, and machine learning, with a particular interest in how intelligent systems can learn from data and interaction to perform diverse tasks in real-world environments.

I am also developing **WatchOut!**, an AI-powered video analysis system that detects suspicious behavior in unmanned stores and generates evidence-based reports with suggested response actions.
